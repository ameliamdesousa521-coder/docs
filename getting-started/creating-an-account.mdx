Creating an account on The Medical Index gives you full access to a curated library of the latest published medical research, personalized weekly update digests, and the ability to contribute your own research papers to the platform. Whether you're a clinician keeping pace with your specialty, a researcher sharing your work, or a curious reader exploring advances across medicine, your free account is the gateway to everything The Medical Index has to offer.

<Note>
  Creating an account is completely free. The Medical Index does not charge any fees to sign up, browse research, or upload papers.
</Note>

## Stay in the Loop with Email Updates

When you create an account, you can opt in to email notifications that keep you up to date on what's happening across The Medical Index. Email updates can include:

- New research papers posted in your selected specialties
- The weekly research digest curated by our editorial team
- Notifications when papers you've bookmarked are updated or cited
- Platform announcements and new feature releases

You're in full control — choose exactly which emails you want to receive (or none at all) from your [Notifications](/account/notifications) settings at any time.

## Sign Up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to themedicalindex.com and click Sign Up">
    Navigate to [themedicalindex.com](https://themedicalindex.com) and click the **Sign Up** button in the top-right corner of the homepage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your name, email address, and a password">
    Fill in your full name, a valid email address, and a secure password. Your password must be at least eight characters long and include at least one number or special character.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your email address">
    Check your inbox for a confirmation email from The Medical Index and click the verification link inside. If the email doesn't arrive within a few minutes, check your spam folder or request a new link from the sign-up page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete your profile and select your specialties">
    Once verified, you'll be prompted to complete your profile. Choose the medical specialties you're most interested in — this tailors your research feed and weekly updates to the topics that matter most to you. You can update your selections at any time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You're in — explore the research feed">
    Your account is ready. Head to your personalized research feed to start discovering the latest publications, browse by specialty, and subscribe to weekly update digests.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Email verification is required before you can upload research papers to the platform. Make sure you complete the verification step so your contributor access is fully activated.
</Note>

## Account Types

The Medical Index offers two levels of access, designed to suit different roles on the platform.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Reader / Contributor">
    The standard account type, free for everyone. As a reader and contributor you can:

    - Browse and search the full research library
    - Subscribe to weekly specialty update digests
    - Bookmark papers and save custom searches
    - Upload and publish your own research papers (available after email verification)
    - Manage your profile, notification preferences, and specialty interests
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Admin">
    Admin access is **invite-only** and is granted to team members responsible for managing the platform's editorial and distribution workflows. In addition to all standard account capabilities, admins have access to:

    - The **Weekly Research Update Management Panel**, where they can curate, schedule, and send weekly digests to subscribers
    - Platform-wide content moderation tools
    - Contributor management and paper review queues

    If you believe your role warrants admin access, reach out to your organization's existing admin or contact The Medical Index support team.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Tip>
  You can update your specialties, notification preferences, and profile details at any time by visiting your [Profile Settings](/account/profile-settings) page.
</Tip>
