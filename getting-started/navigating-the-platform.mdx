The Medical Index is organized around four core areas: a live research feed that surfaces the latest published papers, a specialty browser for drilling into specific medical disciplines, an upload portal for contributing your own work, and a personal dashboard for managing your account and notifications. This guide walks you through each section so you can move around the platform with confidence from day one.

## Platform Sections

  <Card title="Research Feed" icon="newspaper">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Specialty Browser" icon="list-tree">
    Explore research organized by medical discipline. The Specialty Browser lets you navigate the full catalog filtered by specialty — from Cardiology and Oncology to Neurology, Infectious Disease, and beyond.   <Card title="Upload Portal" icon="arrow-up-from-bracket">

  <Card title="Account Dashboard" icon="circle-user">

## Using the Search Bar

The search bar is available at the top of every page and is the fastest way to find specific research on the platform. You can search across multiple dimensions to narrow your results quickly.

<Accordion title="Search by keyword">
  Type any term — a condition, treatment, drug name, or concept — into the search bar and press **Enter**. The platform searches across paper titles, abstracts, and tags to return the most relevant results.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Search by author">
  To find papers by a specific researcher, enter their name in the search bar. For best results with common names, include an initial or institutional affiliation alongside the name (for example, *"J. Smith cardiology"*).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Search by specialty">
  Enter a medical specialty directly into the search bar to see all papers tagged under that discipline. You can also combine a specialty with a keyword for more focused results — for example, *"oncology immunotherapy"*.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Search by publication date">
  Use date filters to scope your search to a specific time window. After entering a search term, select the **Date** filter in the results toolbar to set a from/to range and surface only the most recent — or historically relevant — research.
</Accordion>

## Filtering by Specialty

The specialty filter is the quickest way to tune out noise and focus on the research that matters to your practice or field. Every specialty available on the platform has its own dedicated page, complete with the latest papers, top contributors, and a subscription option for weekly digest updates.

Browse the full list of available specialties and subscribe to the ones most relevant to you at [/updates/specialties](/updates/specialties). Subscribed specialties automatically populate your weekly update emails and surface more prominently in your research feed.

<Tip>
  Save time on repeat visits by bookmarking individual papers or saving your most-used searches. Open any paper and select **Bookmark** to add it to your saved library in the Account Dashboard. To save a search, run your query and select **Save this search** from the results toolbar — the platform will notify you whenever new papers match your saved criteria.
</Tip>
