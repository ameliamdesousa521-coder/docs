Your profile settings page is your central hub for managing everything tied to your Medical Index account. From here you can update your personal details, change your login credentials, tailor your specialty preferences to sharpen your research feed, and — if needed — request account deletion.

## Updating Your Profile

Keep your name, institution, and bio current so other researchers and editors can identify your work accurately.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your account menu">
    Click your avatar or initials in the top-right corner of any page, then select **Profile Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit your details">
    Click **Edit Profile** and update your **Full Name**, **Institution / Affiliation**, and **Bio** fields as needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Click **Save Changes**. A confirmation banner will appear at the top of the page once your profile is updated.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Changing Your Email or Password

You can update your login credentials at any time from the **Account Security** section of your profile settings.

**To change your email address:**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Account Security">
    From **Profile Settings**, scroll to the **Account Security** section and click **Change Email**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your new email">
    Type your new email address, confirm it in the second field, and then enter your current password to authorise the change.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your new address">
    Check your new inbox for a verification email from The Medical Index and click **Confirm Email Address**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Changing your email address puts your account into an unverified state until you complete the confirmation step. You will not be able to upload or submit research until your new email address has been verified.
</Warning>

**To change your password:**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Account Security">
    From **Profile Settings**, scroll to the **Account Security** section and click **Change Password**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your current and new passwords">
    Provide your current password, then enter and confirm your new password. Passwords must be at least 8 characters and include a mix of letters and numbers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your new password">
    Click **Update Password**. You will be asked to sign in again with your new credentials.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Specialty Preferences

Setting your specialty preferences tells The Medical Index which areas of research matter most to you. The platform uses these preferences to personalise your research feed, surface relevant new papers, and tailor the content highlighted in your weekly digest.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Specialty Preferences">
    From **Profile Settings**, click the **Specialty Preferences** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your specialties">
    Browse or search the list of available medical specialties and toggle on each one that applies to your work or interests. You can select as many as you like.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your selections">
    Click **Save Preferences**. Your research feed will begin reflecting your updated specialties immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Deleting Your Account

Account deletion is a permanent action. Before proceeding, make sure you have saved any research or data you wish to keep.

<Warning>
  Deleting your account is irreversible. All of your submitted research, saved papers, and account data will be permanently removed from The Medical Index and cannot be recovered.
</Warning>

To request account deletion, contact the support team directly — deletion is handled manually to ensure the process is intentional and secure.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Contact support">
    Visit [Contact Support](/help/contact-support) and send a message with the subject line **"Account Deletion Request"**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Include your account details">
    Provide the email address associated with your account and confirm that you understand the deletion is permanent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Await confirmation">
    The support team will process your request within 1–2 business days and send you a final confirmation once your account has been deleted.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You can control which emails and platform alerts you receive without deleting your account. Visit [Notifications](/account/notifications) to manage your preferences.
</Tip>
