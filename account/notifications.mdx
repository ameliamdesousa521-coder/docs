The Medical Index notification system gives you precise control over how and when you hear from us. You can opt into research-focused alerts that keep you current with your specialties, subscribe to the weekly digest, and manage essential account and security notifications — all from a single settings panel.

## Notification Types

The table below describes every notification available on the platform, along with its default state.

| Notification | Description | Default |
|---|---|---|
| Weekly Research Digest | A curated weekly email featuring the top papers across medical specialties, selected by the editorial team. | Off |
| New Papers in Your Specialties | An alert sent each time a new paper is published in one of your selected specialty areas. | Off |
| Submission Status Updates | An email confirming when your uploaded paper has been received, is under review, or has been approved or returned. | On |
| Account Activity | Login alerts and security notifications, including new sign-ins from unrecognised devices. | On |

## Managing Notifications

You can turn any notification on or off at any time from your account settings.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your account menu">
    Click your avatar or initials in the top-right corner of any page and select **Profile Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Notifications">
    Click the **Notifications** tab within your settings page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle your preferences">
    Use the toggle switches next to each notification type to turn them on or off. Changes take effect immediately — there is no separate save button.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Unsubscribing from Emails

If you prefer not to manage preferences through your account, you can unsubscribe directly from any marketing or digest email. Every email from The Medical Index includes an **Unsubscribe** link in the footer — click it to be removed from that mailing list instantly.

Alternatively, follow the steps above to turn off specific notification types from within your account settings.

<Note>
  Unsubscribing from marketing or research emails (such as the Weekly Research Digest) does **not** affect your account security alerts. Login notifications and account activity emails will continue to be sent to keep your account safe, regardless of your other preferences.
</Note>
