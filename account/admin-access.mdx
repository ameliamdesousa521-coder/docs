Admin access is a special, invite-only privilege on The Medical Index. It is granted directly by the platform owner and unlocks a dedicated set of tools that standard accounts cannot see or use. With admin access, you can manage the weekly research update section — drafting, curating, and publishing the digest that goes out to all subscribers — as well as oversee the editorial review queue for submitted papers.

## What Admins Can Do

Admin accounts have access to a range of editorial and platform-management capabilities that go beyond standard user permissions:

- **Curate and compose the weekly digest** before it is sent to subscribers, including selecting featured papers and writing editorial summaries.
- **Publish or schedule the weekly update post**, choosing an immediate send or a future delivery date.
- **Feature or pin specific research papers** to highlight important or high-impact work across the platform.
- **Manage submitted research in the editorial review queue**, including approving, requesting revisions on, or declining submissions.
- **View the admin dashboard overview** — see a summary of recent submissions, pending review items, digest status, and platform activity at a glance.

## The Weekly Update Panel

The weekly update panel is an admin-only workspace where each issue of the digest is drafted, refined, and published. It is not visible or accessible to standard account holders.

Use the following steps each week to compose and send the digest to subscribers:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in with your admin account">
    Go to the platform sign-in page and enter your admin email and password. Admin accounts use the same sign-in flow as standard accounts but unlock additional navigation items upon login.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to the Admin Dashboard">
    After signing in, click the **Admin Dashboard** link that appears in your account menu to access the admin panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Weekly Updates">
    In the Admin Dashboard sidebar, click **Weekly Updates** to open the updates management panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new update">
    Click **New Update** to open a blank draft for this week's digest.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build the digest">
    Add featured papers by searching the platform or pasting DOI links, write short editorial summaries for each entry, and set the intended delivery date and time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview the email">
    Click **Preview** to see exactly how the digest will appear in subscribers' inboxes. Review formatting, links, and content carefully before proceeding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish and send">
    When you are satisfied with the preview, click **Publish & Send**. The digest will be delivered immediately (or at the scheduled time) to all subscribers who have opted in to the Weekly Research Digest.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Requesting Admin Access

Admin accounts are not self-service — they are created and granted by the platform owner on a case-by-case basis. If you believe you should have admin access, reach out to the team and explain your role and intended use.

To request access, visit [Contact Support](/help/contact-support) and send a message with the subject line **"Admin Access Request"**, including your account email and a brief description of why you need admin-level access.

<Warning>
  Admin credentials must never be shared with another person. Each admin has their own individual account. If you suspect your admin credentials have been compromised, contact support immediately and mark your message as **"URGENT: Security"**.
</Warning>

<Note>
  The weekly update panel and all other admin-only tools are invisible to standard accounts. If you are signed in with a standard account, you will not see the Admin Dashboard link or any admin navigation items — this is expected behaviour, not a technical issue.
</Note>
