The weekly digest is a curated email newsletter published by The Medical Index admin team every week. Rather than asking you to comb through every new paper yourself, the admins review the week's incoming research across all specialties and select the most significant, high-impact findings to feature. As a subscriber, you receive a concise, well-organized summary in your inbox on a consistent schedule — giving you a reliable way to stay informed even during the busiest weeks.

## What's in the Digest

Each edition of the weekly digest is structured to give you both breadth and depth across current medical research.

- **Top research picks across specialties** — the admin team selects standout papers from a wide range of medical disciplines, so you won't miss important developments outside your primary focus area.
- **Brief summaries of key findings** — every featured paper includes a plain-language summary of the core findings, so you can quickly judge whether a paper warrants deeper reading.
- **Links to full papers** — each summary links directly to the paper's detail page on The Medical Index, where you can read the abstract, access the full text (when available), and save the paper to your library.
- **Specialty highlights** — the digest groups selected papers by medical specialty, making it easy to skip straight to the areas most relevant to you.

## How to Subscribe

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in to your account">
    Go to [themeditcalindex.com](/) and sign in with your registered email address and password. You must have an active account to manage notification preferences.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Account Settings">
    Click your profile avatar in the top-right corner of any page, then select **Account Settings** from the dropdown menu. Navigate to the **Notifications** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable the Weekly Research Digest">
    In the **Email Notifications** section, find the **Weekly Research Digest** toggle and switch it on. The toggle turns green when the subscription is active.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your preferred delivery day">
    If a delivery day preference is shown, select the day of the week you'd like to receive the digest from the dropdown. This lets you schedule it for a time that fits your routine.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm and save">
    Click **Save Preferences** at the bottom of the Notifications page. You'll see a confirmation message, and your first digest will arrive on the next scheduled delivery day.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The weekly digest is curated and published exclusively by The Medical Index admin team. Subscribers receive the digest as a read-only email — individual users cannot edit, reorder, or submit papers to a specific digest edition. If you believe a paper should be featured, contact the admin team through the platform's support channel.
</Note>

<Tip>
  You can review and update all your notification preferences — including digest delivery day and specialty alert settings — at any time in your [Notification Settings](/account/notifications).
</Tip>
