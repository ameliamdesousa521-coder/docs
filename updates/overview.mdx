The Medical Index updates system is your central hub for staying informed about the latest advances in medical research. As new papers are published and added to the platform, the updates feed surfaces them in real time — so whether you're tracking breakthroughs in oncology, following a specific author, or simply keeping pace with your field, you'll always have a clear picture of what's new. The weekly digest and specialty filters work alongside the feed to give you both a curated summary and the precision tools to find exactly what matters to you.

## How Research Gets Added

New research is added to The Medical Index on a regular basis, drawing from a wide range of peer-reviewed journals and medical publications. Each paper is tagged with relevant specialties, author information, and publication metadata as it enters the platform. In addition to the live feed, the platform's admin team reviews incoming research each week and hand-picks a selection of the most significant findings to feature in the weekly digest. This two-layer approach means you can either browse the full stream of new publications at your own pace or rely on expert curation to surface the most noteworthy work.

<Note>
  The public updates feed is available to all registered users. Sign in to your account to start browsing the latest research, save papers, and subscribe to the weekly digest.
</Note>
