Specialties are the core organizing principle behind The Medical Index's research library. Every paper on the platform is tagged with one or more medical disciplines at the time it's submitted, making it possible to navigate thousands of publications without wading through irrelevant content. Whether you're a specialist looking for depth in a single field or a generalist who needs a broad view across disciplines, specialty filters let you shape the research feed into exactly the lens you need.

## Available Specialties

The Medical Index covers a wide and growing range of medical disciplines. Use the specialty browser or feed filters to explore research in any of the following areas.

## Setting Your Specialty Preferences

You can personalize your research feed by telling The Medical Index which specialties matter most to you. When you set specialty preferences in your profile, the platform prioritizes matching papers in your default feed and surfaces them more prominently in the **Most Relevant** sort view.

To set your specialty preferences:

1. Click your profile avatar in the top-right corner and select **Account Settings**.
2. Navigate to the **Research Preferences** tab.
3. Under **My Specialties**, select all disciplines that are relevant to your work. You can choose as many as you like.
4. Click **Save Preferences**.

Your feed will immediately begin reflecting your selections. You can update your specialty preferences at any time without affecting your search or filter settings.

## Submitting Research in a Specialty

When you upload a paper to The Medical Index, you are prompted to assign it to one or more specialties during the submission process. Accurate specialty tagging is important — it determines how the paper is indexed and which users' feeds and filters it appears in. Select every specialty that genuinely describes the paper's subject matter, but avoid over-tagging with loosely related disciplines.

For a full guide to the upload process, including specialty selection, see [How to Upload Research](/uploading/how-to-upload).

<Tip>
  Choosing accurate specialty tags when uploading your research helps it get discovered by the right audience. Papers tagged to relevant specialties appear in filtered feeds, specialty highlight sections of the weekly digest, and the research lists of users who have set matching preferences.
</Tip>
