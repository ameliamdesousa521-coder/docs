The Medical Index support team is here to help you with any issue you encounter on the platform — whether that's trouble with your account, a question about uploading research, a technical error, or anything else. This page explains the best way to reach us and what to include in your message so we can resolve your issue as quickly as possible.

## Before You Contact Us

Many common questions are already answered in our documentation. Checking there first often gets you a faster answer than waiting for a reply.

<Tip>
  Visit the [FAQ](/help/faq) for quick answers to the most common questions about accounts, uploads, the weekly digest, and platform access. You can also browse the relevant documentation section — for example, [Profile Settings](/account/profile-settings) for account questions or [Notifications](/account/notifications) for email preferences.
</Tip>

## Ways to Reach Us

The primary way to contact the support team is by email:

**Email:** [support@themedicalindex.com](mailto:support@themedicalindex.com)

**Response time:** The team typically responds within **1–2 business days**. Messages sent over the weekend or on public holidays will be picked up on the next working day.

## What to Include in Your Message

The more detail you provide upfront, the faster we can diagnose and resolve your issue. When writing to support, include the following:

- **Your account email address** — so we can locate your account quickly.
- **A clear description of the issue** — explain what you were trying to do and what happened instead.
- **Steps you have already tried** — this helps us avoid suggesting things you've already done.
- **Any error messages you received** — copy the exact wording of the error, or attach a screenshot if that's easier.
- **For upload issues:** include the title of the paper and the file format you were trying to upload.
