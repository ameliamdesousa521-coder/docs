This FAQ covers the questions our users ask most often about The Medical Index. Browse the topics below to find quick answers about creating an account, uploading research, managing your preferences, and understanding how the platform works. If you can't find what you're looking for here, the [support team](/help/contact-support) is always happy to help.

<AccordionGroup>

  <Accordion title="Is The Medical Index free to use?">
    Yes. Creating an account and browsing research on The Medical Index is completely free. There are no subscription fees or paywalls for standard platform access. Some individual papers may be subject to the original publisher's licensing terms, but accessing them through The Medical Index itself costs nothing.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need an account to read research?">
    You need a free account to access the full platform and read research papers. Sign-up takes only a few minutes — visit the registration page, enter your email address and a password, verify your email, and you're in. Browsing the platform without an account is not currently supported.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I upload any research paper?">
    Not every paper is eligible for submission. All uploaded research must meet the platform's submission guidelines, which cover topics such as originality, formatting, supported file types, and ethical standards. Review the full criteria on the [Submission Guidelines](/uploading/submission-guidelines) page before uploading.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I subscribe to the weekly digest?">
    You can subscribe to the Weekly Research Digest from your account settings. Go to **Profile Settings → Notifications** and toggle on **Weekly Research Digest**. You can also learn more about what the digest contains and how it is curated by visiting the [Weekly Research Digest](/updates/weekly-digest) page.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How long does it take for my paper to be reviewed?">
    The editorial team typically reviews submitted papers within **2–5 business days**. You will receive an email notification at each stage of the review process — when your submission is received, when it is under active review, and when a decision has been made. Make sure **Submission Status Updates** are enabled in your [Notifications](/account/notifications) settings so you don't miss these emails.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What file formats are supported for uploads?">
    The Medical Index currently accepts uploads in the following formats: **PDF**, **DOCX**, and **DOI links** (which point to a paper already hosted by an external publisher). For a full breakdown of format requirements and file size limits, see the [Supported Formats](/uploading/supported-formats) page.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I upload preprints?">
    Yes, preprints are welcome on The Medical Index. However, they must be clearly labelled as preprints at the time of submission. The editorial team will include a preprint indicator on the paper's listing so that readers are aware the work has not yet completed formal peer review.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I change my specialty preferences?">
    You can update your specialty preferences at any time from your account settings. Go to **Profile Settings → Specialty Preferences**, select or deselect the specialties relevant to you, and save your changes. Your research feed will update immediately. Visit [Profile Settings](/account/profile-settings) for step-by-step instructions.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is admin access?">
    Admin access is an invite-only privilege granted by the platform owner. Admins can curate and publish the weekly research digest, feature or pin specific papers, and manage the editorial review queue for submitted research. Standard users do not have access to these tools. If you need admin access, [contact support](/help/contact-support) to make a request.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I delete my account?">
    Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone — all your submitted research and account data will be removed. To request deletion, [contact the support team](/help/contact-support) with the subject line **"Account Deletion Request"** and include the email address on your account. The team will process your request within 1–2 business days.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I didn't receive my verification email — what should I do?">
    First, check your spam or junk folder, as verification emails are occasionally filtered by email providers. If the email isn't there, wait a few minutes and check again. You can also request a new verification email from the sign-in page by clicking **Resend Verification Email**. If you still don't receive it after trying these steps, [contact support](/help/contact-support) and include the email address you registered with.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I share or embed research papers?">
    You can share a direct link to any paper on The Medical Index with anyone — linked papers are accessible to users with a platform account. Embedding a paper (for example, on an external website or blog) depends on the licensing terms set by the original publisher. The Medical Index does not override or grant additional rights beyond those the publisher has already established. Check the paper's listed license or contact the publisher directly for embedding permissions.
  </Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
