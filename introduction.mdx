## How It Works

Get up and running with The Medical Index in four simple steps.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Your Free Account">
    Head to the sign-up page and register for a free account. All you need is a valid email address. Once verified, you will have immediate access to the full research catalogue and all platform features.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore Research by Specialty">
    Use the search bar or browse by specialty to discover peer-reviewed papers relevant to your practice or study. Apply filters for publication date, journal, or author to narrow your results and surface the most relevant content.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Subscribe to the Weekly Digest">
    Select the medical specialties you care about most and subscribe to the weekly digest. Every week you will receive a concise, expertly curated email highlighting the most impactful new research across your chosen fields.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload and Share Your Research (Optional)">
    If you have published research to share, upload your paper to The Medical Index to reach a wider medical audience. Follow the submission guidelines to ensure your work is indexed correctly and discoverable by the community.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  **Manage your Weekly Digest preferences at any time.** Visit the [Weekly Digest](/updates/weekly-digest) section of your account settings to add or remove specialties, adjust delivery frequency, or pause your subscription whenever you need to.
</Tip>
