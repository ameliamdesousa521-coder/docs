The Medical Index accepts research submissions in three formats: PDF, Microsoft Word (DOCX), and DOI links to externally hosted papers. PDF is the recommended format because it preserves your document's layout and typography exactly as intended. DOCX files are also accepted and are automatically converted to PDF upon upload. If your paper is already published online with a Digital Object Identifier (DOI), you can submit the link directly — no file upload needed. Accepting multiple formats ensures that researchers can contribute regardless of how their work was originally prepared or published.

## Supported Formats at a Glance

| Format | Details | Max File Size |
|---|---|---|
| **PDF** | Recommended format; preserves all original formatting and typography. Text must be selectable (not a scanned image). | 25 MB |
| **DOCX** | Microsoft Word format; automatically converted to PDF during the upload process. Ensure all embedded images and tables are intact before uploading. | 25 MB |
| **DOI Link** | A permanent link to a paper published on an external journal or repository. No file upload required — paste the DOI URL directly into the submission form. | N/A |

## Preparing Your PDF

Follow these steps to make sure your PDF is ready for review and readable on the platform:

- **Use selectable text.** Export your PDF from a word processor or typesetting tool (such as LaTeX or Microsoft Word) rather than scanning a printed document. Text must be machine-readable so the platform can index it for search.
- **Include a cover page.** Add a first page that clearly shows the paper's title, full author list, and abstract. This helps reviewers and readers identify your work at a glance.
- **Embed all fonts.** When exporting to PDF, choose the option to embed fonts so the document renders correctly for all readers.
- **Check page size and margins.** Use standard page dimensions (A4 or US Letter) with reasonable margins so the document is legible on all screen sizes.
- **Remove password protection.** Encrypted or password-protected PDFs cannot be processed — ensure your file is unrestricted before uploading.

<Warning>
  Scanned image-only PDFs — where each page is a photograph of printed text rather than real digital text — are likely to be rejected during editorial review. The platform cannot index the content of image-based PDFs, which makes the paper unsearchable. Always export directly from your source document instead of scanning.
</Warning>

## Preparing Your DOCX

- Confirm that all images, charts, and tables are embedded in the file and not linked from an external location.
- Use standard heading styles so the document structure is preserved after conversion to PDF.
- Remove any tracked changes or comments before uploading — only the final accepted version should be submitted.

## Using a DOI Link

A **Digital Object Identifier (DOI)** is a permanent, standardized URL assigned to a published academic paper. It typically looks like `https://doi.org/10.1000/xyz123`. Submitting a DOI is the fastest option when your paper is already available through a journal or preprint server.

**How to find a DOI:**

- **PubMed** — search for your paper at [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov). The DOI is listed in the citation details panel.
- **The journal's website** — open the paper's abstract or landing page on the publisher's site. The DOI is usually displayed near the top of the page or in the article metadata.
- **Your accepted manuscript** — many journals include the DOI in the acceptance or publication confirmation email.

Once you have the DOI, paste the full URL (beginning with `https://doi.org/`) into the DOI field in the submission form. The platform will fetch the paper's metadata automatically where possible, though you should verify and complete any fields that are not populated.

<Tip>
  A valid DOI or well-formatted file is only one part of a successful submission. Make sure your paper also meets the content and quality standards described on the [Submission Guidelines](/uploading/submission-guidelines) page before you submit.
</Tip>
