Any verified account holder can contribute to The Medical Index by uploading medical research papers directly to the platform. Submissions help keep the community informed, expand the breadth of available research across specialties, and make important findings more discoverable. Once you submit a paper, our editorial team reviews it for relevance and formatting before it goes live — making your work accessible to clinicians, researchers, and other subscribers worldwide.

## Prerequisites

<Note>
  Before you begin, make sure you have the following ready:

  - **Verified email address** — your account must be email-verified before you can submit.
  - **Logged-in account** — you must be signed in to access the Upload Portal.
  - **Paper in a supported format** — PDF, DOCX, or a valid DOI link. See [Supported Formats](/uploading/supported-formats) for details.
</Note>

## Upload Process

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in and navigate to the Upload Portal">
    Log in to your account at The Medical Index. Once signed in, find the **Upload Portal** in the top navigation bar, or click the **Upload Research** button on your dashboard home page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click New Submission">
    Inside the Upload Portal, click the **New Submission** button to open a fresh submission form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your file or provide a DOI">
    Attach your research paper by dragging and dropping your file into the upload area, clicking **Browse Files** to select a PDF or DOCX from your device, or pasting a **DOI link** if the paper is already published online. Only one file or link is accepted per submission.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the paper metadata">
    Complete all required metadata fields for your submission:

    - **Title** — the full title of the paper as published.
    - **Authors** — list all authors in order, separated by commas.
    - **Abstract** — paste or type the paper's abstract.
    - **Journal / Publication** — the name of the journal or conference where the paper appeared (or "Preprint" if applicable).
    - **Publication date** — the date the paper was originally published or posted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select one or more specialties">
    Tag your submission with the medical specialty or specialties it covers — for example, Cardiology, Oncology, or Infectious Disease. Accurate tagging helps subscribers in those specialties discover your paper through search and weekly digests.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your submission details">
    Read through everything on the summary screen before you submit. Verify that the file has uploaded correctly, the metadata is accurate, and the specialties are appropriate. Use the **Edit** links to correct any section if needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Submit for Review">
    When you are satisfied with your submission, click the **Submit for Review** button. Your paper enters the editorial review queue immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch for your confirmation email">
    Within a few minutes of submitting, you will receive an email confirming that your paper has been received. Your paper will appear on The Medical Index once the editorial review is complete.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After Submission

Once you submit a paper, it enters The Medical Index editorial review queue. The review team checks each submission for:

- **Relevance** — the paper must relate to human health, medicine, or biomedical research.
- **Format compliance** — the file must be readable and complete, with all required metadata present.
- **Duplicate detection** — submissions are checked against existing content to avoid redundancy.
- **Content standards** — the paper must meet the platform's submission guidelines.

Most reviews are completed within **2–3 business days**. If the team has questions or requires changes, you will be contacted at the email address linked to your account. Papers that do not meet the guidelines are returned with an explanation so you can revise and resubmit.

<Tip>
  Before submitting, review the full content standards and quality requirements on the [Submission Guidelines](/uploading/submission-guidelines) page to improve your chances of a smooth review.
</Tip>

<Note>
  You will receive an email notification as soon as your paper is published on The Medical Index. The email includes a direct link to your paper's listing page, which you can share with colleagues.
</Note>
